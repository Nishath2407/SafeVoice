About SafeVoice

Built with one purpose — to ensure no woman ever faces trauma and harassment alone in silence.

Our Mission

SafeVoice uses artificial intelligence to detect emotional distress in women experiencing harassment-related trauma, and connects them — anonymously — with the right level of support. Because asking for help should never require risking your identity, your job, or your safety.

🔒

Radical Anonymity

No name. No face. No email required. Every session gets a random ID. Your identity is never stored.

🤖

AI-Powered Insight

We use state-of-the-art emotion AI to understand your pain beyond just words and find what you need.

💚

Human Connection

Technology opens the door. Real, licensed therapists walk through it with you — on your terms.

🆘

Immediate Safety

High-risk cases are automatically escalated to emergency responders. No one falls through the cracks.
